---

# 🎯 Business Questions

### Q1 — Which region is carrying the business?

Identify the region generating the highest total revenue across January–June 2026.

### Q2 — Is revenue momentum improving?

Calculate month-over-month revenue growth for:

- April → May
- May → June

Then determine the overall direction.

### Q3 — Should UrbanKart pause its weakest sales channel?

Identify the lowest-revenue channel and evaluate whether revenue alone is enough to justify shutting it down.

This makes South UrbanKart's strongest regional revenue contributor and an important region for continued growth and customer retention.

---

## 2. Revenue Momentum

Revenue showed positive momentum in the most recent months.

### April → May

**+4.4%**

### May → June

**+25.0%**

### Overall Direction

**Growing 📈**

The acceleration in June is particularly important because growth increased substantially compared with the previous month.

---

# 🛒 3. Sales Channel Analysis

Marketplace generated the lowest total revenue among UrbanKart's three channels.

### Channel Revenue

| Channel | Revenue | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| App | ₹16,189,317 | 40.1% |
| Website | ₹13,520,258 | 33.5% |
| Marketplace | ₹10,634,197 | 26.4% |

However, **lowest revenue does not automatically mean weakest business performance**.

Marketplace generated approximately:

> **₹10,840 revenue per order**

Compared with:

- Website — ₹8,072/order
- App — ₹7,481/order

This means Marketplace has the **highest revenue per order** despite having the lowest total revenue.

---

# 💡 COO Recommendation

### Recommendation: **Do NOT pause Marketplace.**

Marketplace is the lowest-revenue channel at ₹10.63M and represents 26.4% of total revenue, but the data does not support shutting it down based on revenue alone.

Its economics are stronger on a revenue-per-order basis, generating approximately **₹10,840 per order**, compared with **₹8,072 for Website** and **₹7,481 for App**.

Marketplace is also showing positive momentum. Revenue increased from approximately **₹1.81M in April to ₹2.20M in May and ₹2.59M in June**, while orders increased from **182 to 201 to 236** over the same period.

The channel therefore appears to be growing rather than declining.

**Instead of pausing Marketplace, I would review channel-level costs and margins, then monitor Marketplace growth, order volume, and profitability next quarter before making a shutdown decision.**
